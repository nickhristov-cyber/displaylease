About DisplayLease

30 Years of Display & Technology Expertise

DisplayLease is built on three decades of hands-on experience in advanced electronic and display systems — including CCTV & IP cameras, video display and audio systems, data and communication networks, wireless connectivity, and access control integration.

This deep technical foundation made the expansion into large-format LED display systems a natural evolution — offering businesses across Malta a complete solution for informative, promotional, branding and advertising purposes in retail, conventions, political campaigns and high-exposure initiatives.
