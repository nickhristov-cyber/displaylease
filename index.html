About DisplayLease

30 Years of Display & Technology Expertise

Operating across Malta and Gozo, DisplayLease is built on 30 years of continuous expertise in advanced electronic and display systems. Our background spans:

ELV (Extra Low Voltage) systems design & installation
Access control system integration
Surveillance cameras — CCTV & IP camera systems
Video display & professional audio systems
Data networks & wireless connectivity solutions

This deep technical foundation made the expansion into large-format LED display systems a natural evolution — offering businesses across Malta and Gozo a complete solution for informative, promotional, branding and advertising purposes in retail, conventions, political campaigns and high-exposure initiatives.
